Screen Agent

The agent looks at your main screen, then clicks and presses any key or shortcut (except Esc, which is your emergency stop). It needs a vision model and the app opened with local_ai_app.bat. Hold Esc at any time to stop it. Pick one group at a time. Create groups in the AI Groups tab. With a group, the agent uses its AIs instead of the chat model and the decision model below. The model that chooses clicks and key presses. Automatic uses the chat model, or a text model such as qwen2.5 when the chat model is a tiny picture model like moondream (which then only describes the screen).

Text-to-Speech

Reads the Screen Agent's reasoning and actions aloud while it runs. Press Ctrl+. or the red Stop speaking button to silence it instantly.

AI Groups

A group is a team of AIs. In + New Conversation the AIs of a group chat with each other (give each a display name and avatar). For the Screen Agent, give each AI a job: sees the screen, decides actions, or does both; if one fails the next with the same job takes over. Make as many groups as you like.

Hardware / Camera

Choose the webcam the Screen Agent and Call AI use when their camera is on. The list shows every connected camera, including virtual ones such as OBS Virtual Cam.

Call AI

Call AI works inside a Text AI chat: open a chat and press Call AI (or use its + menu) to start a voice call with that contact. Talk normally and the AI answers out loud. The browser's speech service turns your voice into text, and in Edge that needs an internet connection. With the camera on, every time you speak the AI also gets a snapshot from your webcam and is told it comes from your webcam. How long it waits after you stop talking.
